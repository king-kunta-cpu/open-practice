How to choose a model in 20 minutes

  1. Write three prompts from your real work.
  2. Run each on two or three models (free keys from our guides).
  3. Score each answer: correct, complete, clear, follows format (0–2 each).
  4. Note speed and any errors or refusals.
  5. Pick the cheapest model that scores well — not the most famous.
