How to get a free Groq API key

The easiest first key: email signup, no card, very fast open models.

Checked 29 October 2026. Free tiers change — confirm the live numbers in the console.

Sign up ↗ Official quickstart ↗ Live limits / pricing ↗

You need
Email or Google/GitHub sign-in. No card.
Free allowance
Free plan with per-model rate limits (commonly about 30 requests per minute; daily caps differ by model — from about 1,000 to 14,400 requests per day). Live numbers are on the Limits page in the console.
Best for
Every free tool on this site. Fast drafts, chatbots, Telegram/Discord bots.
Region notes
Groq is a US company and blocks countries under US sanctions. Most of Africa, Asia and Latin America can sign up. If the console says your region is unsupported, use OpenRouter or Gemini instead.
Key looks like
gsk_…

Step by step

  1. Open console.groq.com and choose “Sign up” — email, Google or GitHub all work.
  2. Confirm your email if asked. You land on the Playground.
  3. Click “API Keys” in the left menu, then “Create API Key”.
  4. Name it after where it will be used, e.g. “open-practice-browser”. Click Submit.
  5. Copy the key now — it starts with gsk_ and is shown only once. Paste it into a password manager or a private note.
  6. On Open Practice, open Key settings, choose Groq, paste the key and press “Test key”. You should see a list of models.

Use it on Open Practice

Paste the key into Key settings and press Test key. Then open any free tool and choose “Draft with my AI key”. The key stays in your browser.

If something goes wrong

401 / invalid API keyYou copied part of the key or it was deleted. Create a new one.
429 rate limitYou hit the per-minute or daily cap. Wait a minute or switch provider.
Model not foundModels are retired from time to time. Press “Test key” to pick a current one.

Keep your key safe

  • Treat a key like a password: never post it in chats, screenshots, GitHub or website code.
  • Create one key per project so you can delete just that one if it leaks.
  • If a key leaks, delete it in the provider console immediately and create a new one.
  • Prefer free-only keys (for example, OpenRouter with a $0 credit limit) so a leak cannot cost money.
  • On a shared or public computer, do not tick “remember on this device”, and press “Forget all keys” when you finish.

Questions people ask

Is Groq free?

Free plan with per-model rate limits (commonly about 30 requests per minute; daily caps differ by model — from about 1,000 to 14,400 requests per day). Live numbers are on the Limits page in the console.

Do I need a credit card for Groq?

Email or Google/GitHub sign-in. No card.

Is Groq available in my country?

Groq is a US company and blocks countries under US sanctions. Most of Africa, Asia and Latin America can sign up. If the console says your region is unsupported, use OpenRouter or Gemini instead.
