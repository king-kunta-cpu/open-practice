FOR COMMUNITY HOSTS / LIBRARIES / ADULT LEARNING GROUPS

Host a small, useful AI taster.

A 60-minute facilitated taster, not a claim that learners completed the three full activities. The complete self-paced starter is estimated at 120 minutes. Materials are free, independent and non-accredited; no partner or session is claimed before agreement.

Download the complete pilot kit ZIP ↓Facilitator plan ↓Anonymous observation sheet ↓

60-minute session

  1. 8 minutes: boundaries, fictional data, privacy and available tool routes.
  2. 12 minutes: test a bounded editor against correct and messy sentences.
  3. 12 minutes: distinguish valid JSON, agreed field types and true source values.
  4. 12 minutes: measure the supplied contrast pairs.
  5. 10 minutes: save one artifact and limitation; sharing is optional.
  6. 6 minutes: collect aggregate friction and choose one next step.

What a host needs

A consenting adult cohort, a named facilitator, an agreed date and venue, and a tested offline fallback. Accounts are optional and this account pilot is 18+. Never ask participants for AI keys, passwords or private documents. Do not upload attendee lists. Download fixtures before relying on connectivity.

What to measure

Attendance, first artifacts kept, the biggest usability obstacle and one improvement. Keep aggregate notes without names. Website counts only include opted-in browsers; do not confuse them with total attendance or mastery.

Invite a host, without a spam sequence

Use the template only after researching a relevant public programme and selecting a named recipient. This kit is not proof of an invitation, booking, partnership or completed session.
