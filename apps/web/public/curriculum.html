How to use this curriculum

Read → inspect the worked example → complete the guided activity → test against expected evidence → write what failed → review your capstone. Fictional fixtures let you practise without private data. Cloud-AI activities use a legitimate service available to you; eligibility varies across regions. Manual alternatives are described, but simulation is not a live integration.

Completion and rubric scores are learner self-reports, not credentials, independent verification or professional accreditation. The capstone threshold is at least 8/10 with 2/2 on the critical safety criterion.
