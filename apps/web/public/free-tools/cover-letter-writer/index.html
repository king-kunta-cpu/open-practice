FREE TOOL · WRITING

Write a cover letter matched to one job

Recruiters skim for evidence that you can do this job. You will paste the key requirements, map each to a real example from your life, and produce a 250–400 word letter that a checker tests for job-title match, evidence and length.

⏱ 10–20 min · Works with or without AI · No signup

You will practise

  • Mapping job requirements to your evidence
  • Writing achievement sentences (action + result)
  • Keeping a letter specific and under one page

No AI key needed. The guided template works for everyone.

    Pro moves
    • One strong number beats three adjectives.
    • Use the same words as the job ad where they are true for you.
    • Save it as PDF with your name in the file name.
    Questions people ask
    How long should a cover letter be?
    Usually 250–400 words — under one page. Recruiters often skim in under a minute.
    Can I reuse one cover letter?
    Reuse the structure, not the letter. Matching evidence to each ad is what makes it work.
    Do I need an AI key to use this?
    No. The guided template teaches the same skill with no AI at all. If you add your own free key (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral or Cerebras), the tool can also draft and revise. The key stays in your browser and requests go straight to that provider.
    Where is my work saved?
    In this browser only (local storage), until you copy or download it. Nothing you type is sent to Open Practice.
