Design an AI workflow with human checkpoints

AI saves time on parts of a task, not usually the whole thing. You will map a recurring task step by step, mark which steps AI drafts and which a human checks, estimate time saved honestly, and write what happens when it goes wrong.

⏱ 10–20 min · Works with or without AI · No signup

You will practise

  • Breaking a task into steps
  • Placing human review where errors are costly
  • Estimating time saved including checking time

    Questions people ask
    What is an AI workflow?
    A repeatable sequence of steps where some are done by AI (drafting, sorting, summarising) and others by people or ordinary tools, with checks in between.
    How do I know AI actually saves time?
    Time the old way, then the new way including checking and fixing. Many “savings” disappear once checking is counted.
    Do I need an AI key to use this?
    No. The guided template teaches the same skill with no AI at all. If you add your own free key (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral or Cerebras), the tool can also draft and revise. The key stays in your browser and requests go straight to that provider.
    Where is my work saved?
    In this browser only (local storage), until you copy or download it. Nothing you type is sent to Open Practice.
