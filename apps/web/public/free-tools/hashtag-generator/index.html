Choose hashtags with a strategy, not a pile

Thirty random hashtags look like spam. A small, deliberate mix — a couple of broad tags, several niche ones, one local and one branded — helps the right people find you. You will build that mix and check its size and format.

⏱ 10–20 min · Works with or without AI · No signup

You will practise

  • Mixing broad, niche, local and branded tags
  • Checking relevance before posting
  • Keeping the count platform-appropriate

    Pro moves
    • Search each tag first: is the content there what you expected?
    • Capitalise words in tags (#CommunityMural) for screen readers.
    • Reuse a core set and rotate the niche tags.
    Questions people ask
    How many hashtags should I use?
    Fewer, relevant tags usually work better than a long list. Many creators use around 3–10 on Instagram and TikTok, and 3–5 on LinkedIn.
    Why capitalise hashtags?
    #CommunityMural is easier to read and screen readers pronounce it correctly; #communitymural is not.
    Do I need an AI key to use this?
    No. The guided template teaches the same skill with no AI at all. If you add your own free key (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral or Cerebras), the tool can also draft and revise. The key stays in your browser and requests go straight to that provider.
    Where is my work saved?
    In this browser only (local storage), until you copy or download it. Nothing you type is sent to Open Practice.
