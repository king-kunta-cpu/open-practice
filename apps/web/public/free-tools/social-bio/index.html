Write a bio that tells people why to follow you

A bio answers three questions in seconds: who are you, what do you offer, why should I follow or contact you? You will write versions for different platforms and check them against real character limits.

⏱ 10–20 min · Works with or without AI · No signup

You will practise

  • Writing a who/what/proof bio formula
  • Fitting platform character limits
  • Adding one clear call to action

No AI key needed. The guided template works for everyone.

    Pro moves
    • Your bio is a signpost, not a CV.
    • Update it when your offer changes.
    • Use the same core sentence across platforms for recognition.
    Questions people ask
    What are the bio character limits?
    At the time of writing: Instagram about 150, TikTok about 80, X 160, WhatsApp Business about 139. Platforms change limits, so check yours when you paste.
    Should I put a link in my bio?
    Yes, if it goes somewhere useful — a booking page, catalogue or WhatsApp chat.
    Do I need an AI key to use this?
    No. The guided template teaches the same skill with no AI at all. If you add your own free key (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral or Cerebras), the tool can also draft and revise. The key stays in your browser and requests go straight to that provider.
    Where is my work saved?
    In this browser only (local storage), until you copy or download it. Nothing you type is sent to Open Practice.
