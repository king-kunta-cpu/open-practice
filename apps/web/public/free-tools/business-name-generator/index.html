    Pro moves
    • Search the national company registry before printing anything.
    • Avoid names that box you into one product or street.
    • Check that the name is not a trademark in your category.
    Questions people ask
    How do I check if a business name is taken?
    Search your country’s company registry, try the domain in a registrar, check social handles, and search the name with your city on Google and on the trademark database.
    Should the domain match exactly?
    It helps, but a short, clear variation (adding your city or a word like “hq”) is fine if the main name is unique.
    Do I need an AI key to use this?
    No. The guided template teaches the same skill with no AI at all. If you add your own free key (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral or Cerebras), the tool can also draft and revise. The key stays in your browser and requests go straight to that provider.
