    Pro moves
    • Use the words recruiters search for, where true.
    • Your first two lines are your elevator pitch.
    • Update after every project with a new number.
    Questions people ask
    How long can a LinkedIn headline be?
    Up to 220 characters at the time of writing. Put your role and the main value in the first part, as it is often cut off.
    What should the About section include?
    What you do and for whom, proof (achievements with numbers), your tools, and what you want people to contact you about.
    Do I need an AI key to use this?
    No. The guided template teaches the same skill with no AI at all. If you add your own free key (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral or Cerebras), the tool can also draft and revise. The key stays in your browser and requests go straight to that provider.
    Where is my work saved?
    In this browser only (local storage), until you copy or download it. Nothing you type is sent to Open Practice.
