Build a landing page for one offer

A landing page has one job: get one kind of visitor to take one action. You will write the headline, problem and benefits, generate a single fast HTML file, check it for clarity and fake proof, and show it to five real people.

⏱ 20–40 min · Download a ZIP · Deploy free · No signup

You will practise

  • Structuring problem → benefits → action
  • Testing a page with real people

No AI key needed. The code comes from a reviewed template.

    Questions people ask

    What should a landing page include?

    A clear headline, who it is for, the problem, 3–5 benefits, proof you can back up, the price if relevant, and one call-to-action button.

    Do I need a form?

    No. A WhatsApp, email or booking link is enough to start and avoids storing personal data.

    Do I need an AI key to use this?

    No. The guided template teaches the same skill with no AI at all. If you add your own free key (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral or Cerebras), the tool can also draft and revise. The key stays in your browser and requests go straight to that provider.

    Where is my work saved?

    In this browser only (local storage), until you copy or download it. Nothing you type is sent to Open Practice.
