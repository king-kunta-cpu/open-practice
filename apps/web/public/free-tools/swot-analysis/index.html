Run a SWOT that leads to decisions

Most SWOTs are lists nobody acts on. You will write each point with evidence, keep internal (strengths, weaknesses) separate from external (opportunities, threats), and end with three actions — which the checker requires.

⏱ 10–20 min · Works with or without AI · No signup

You will practise

  • Separating internal from external factors
  • Backing each point with evidence
  • Turning analysis into 3 actions

No AI key needed. The guided template works for everyone.

    Questions people ask
    What is a SWOT analysis?
    A simple framework listing internal Strengths and Weaknesses and external Opportunities and Threats, used to plan decisions.
    What is the most common SWOT mistake?
    Listing opinions without evidence and never turning the analysis into actions.
    Do I need an AI key to use this?
    No. The guided template teaches the same skill with no AI at all. If you add your own free key (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral or Cerebras), the tool can also draft and revise. The key stays in your browser and requests go straight to that provider.

    In this browser only (local storage), until you copy or download it. Nothing you type is sent to Open Practice.
