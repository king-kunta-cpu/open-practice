FREE TOOL · WRITING

Write a product description that answers buyers’ questions

Online buyers cannot touch your product, so the description must answer what it is, who it is for, what it does for them and the hard facts (size, material, delivery). You will build that from your own facts and test it for benefits, specs and unprovable claims.

⏱ 10–20 min · Works with or without AI · No signup

You will practise

  • Converting features into buyer benefits
  • Writing scannable specs
  • Avoiding claims you cannot prove

    Pro moves
    • Answer the three questions buyers ask in your DMs — in the description.
    • Benefits sell, specs reassure. You need both.
    • Use the words buyers search for, not your internal names.
    Questions people ask
    What makes a good product description?
    It tells the buyer what the product is, who it suits, what it does for them, and the facts — size, material, price and delivery — in a scannable layout.
    How long should it be?
    Most work well at 80–200 words with bullets. Expensive or technical items may need more detail lower down.
    Do I need an AI key to use this?
    No. The guided template teaches the same skill with no AI at all. If you add your own free key (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral or Cerebras), the tool can also draft and revise. The key stays in your browser and requests go straight to that provider.
