Write a cold email that respects the reader

Cold email works when it is about the receiver, short, and easy to say no to. You will research one real detail about the recipient, write under 120 words, and check for honest claims and an opt-out — the habits that keep your domain out of spam folders.

⏱ 10–20 min · Works with or without AI · No signup

You will practise

  • Personalising with one verifiable observation
  • Making a low-friction ask
  • Writing outreach that follows anti-spam basics

    Pro moves
    • Send fewer, better emails: 10 researched beats 500 blasted.
    • Follow up once, a week later, then stop.
    • Laws differ by country — include who you are and honour every opt-out.
    Questions people ask
    Is cold emailing legal?
    In many countries, business-to-business outreach is allowed if you are honest about who you are, have a legitimate reason and honour opt-outs; some countries are stricter. Check the rules where you and your recipients are.
    How many follow-ups should I send?
    One polite follow-up is usually enough. Repeated chasing hurts your reputation and your email deliverability.
    Do I need an AI key to use this?
    No. The guided template teaches the same skill with no AI at all. If you add your own free key (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral or Cerebras), the tool can also draft and revise. The key stays in your browser and requests go straight to that provider.
