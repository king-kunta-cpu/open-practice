Build a prompt with role, task, context and checks

Prompts improve when they read like a brief to a skilled freelancer. You will turn a vague request into a structured prompt — role, task, context, constraints, output format and a self-check — and test it against a rubric.

⏱ 10–20 min · Works with or without AI · No signup

You will practise

  • Writing the 6 parts of a strong prompt
  • Specifying output format precisely
  • Adding constraints that prevent invented facts

    Questions people ask
    What makes a good AI prompt?
    A clear role, one task, the context the AI cannot guess, explicit constraints, the exact output format and an instruction not to invent missing facts.
    Do prompts work the same on every AI?
    No. Structure helps everywhere, but test on the model you will actually use and keep the version that works.
    Do I need an AI key to use this?
    No. The guided template teaches the same skill with no AI at all. If you add your own free key (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral or Cerebras), the tool can also draft and revise. The key stays in your browser and requests go straight to that provider.
    Where is my work saved?
    In this browser only (local storage), until you copy or download it. Nothing you type is sent to Open Practice.
