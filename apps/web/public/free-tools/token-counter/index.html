Estimate tokens before you send a prompt

AI models read and bill in tokens — chunks of roughly four characters of English. Paste a prompt to estimate its tokens and context use, then shrink a bloated prompt in the challenge without losing meaning.

⏱ 5–10 min · Runs in your browser · No signup

You will practise

  • What tokens are
  • Estimating context-window use
  • Writing compact prompts

⚙Use the tool

🔒 Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

✓Practice challenge

Rewrite this prompt in 60 tokens or fewer, keeping “customer”, “refund” and “48”:

I would really like you to please help me write a reply to a customer who has written to us because they are not happy and they want a refund for their order, and I want you to tell them in a very polite way that we will process the refund within 48 hours if they send us the receipt.

✓ Challenge recorded on this device.

Questions people ask

How accurate is this counter?

It is an estimate (about 4 characters per token for English). Exact counts depend on each model’s tokenizer; other languages often use more tokens.

Why do tokens matter?

They decide cost, speed and whether your text fits the model’s context window.
