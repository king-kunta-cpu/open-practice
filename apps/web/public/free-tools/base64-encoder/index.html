Encode and decode Base64 (UTF-8 safe)

Base64 turns bytes into plain text so they can travel in URLs, emails and JSON. It is encoding, not encryption — anyone can decode it. Encode, decode, then crack the challenge message.

Questions people ask

Is Base64 secure?

No. It hides nothing — it is a reversible encoding. Never use it to protect passwords or keys.

What is URL-safe Base64?

A variant that replaces + and / with - and _ and drops = padding so the text is safe in URLs.
