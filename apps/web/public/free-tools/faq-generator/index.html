Build an FAQ from real customer questions

A useful FAQ is built from questions people actually ask — in WhatsApp chats, emails and calls — not guesses. You will list real questions, give the facts, and produce short answers that the checker tests for format, length and the dreaded “contact us for more info” non-answer.

⏱ 10–20 min · Works with or without AI · No signup

You will practise

  • Collecting questions from real conversations
  • Writing answers under 60 words
  • Spotting answers that dodge the question

No AI key needed. The guided template works for everyone.

    Pro moves
    • Put price and refund questions near the top — they are the most asked.
    • Keep a list of new questions from chats and update monthly.
    • Reuse the FAQ in the Chatbot Widget workshop.
    Questions people ask
    How many questions should an FAQ have?
    Start with the 5–10 most common real questions. Add more only when people keep asking them.
    Do FAQs help SEO?
    Clear question-and-answer content can match how people search, but only if it genuinely answers them. Never add fake questions just for search.
    Do I need an AI key to use this?
    No. The guided template teaches the same skill with no AI at all. If you add your own free key (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral or Cerebras), the tool can also draft and revise. The key stays in your browser and requests go straight to that provider.

    In this browser only (local storage), until you copy or download it. Nothing you type is sent to Open Practice.
