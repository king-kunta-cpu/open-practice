FREE TOOL · WRITING

Write a clear email that gets a reply

A good email is a small machine: it tells the reader why they are receiving it, what you need, and by when — in under 150 words. You will brief the situation, draft, and then test the draft for one ask, a deadline, length and tone.

⏱ 10–20 min · Works with or without AI · No signup

You will practise

  • Putting the request in the first two sentences
  • Matching tone to the relationship
  • Cutting an email to under 150 words

No AI key needed. The guided template works for everyone.

    Pro moves
    • If you need two things, consider two emails.
    • Dates beat words like “soon” or “ASAP”.
    • Write the subject line as if it were the only thing they read.
    Questions people ask
    How long should a work email be?
    For a request, aim for under 150 words. If it needs more, add a short summary at the top and details below or in an attachment.
    Should I use AI for sensitive emails?
    Draft with care: do not paste private details about other people into any AI service you do not trust. The guided template lets you write without sending text anywhere.
    Do I need an AI key to use this?
    No. The guided template teaches the same skill with no AI at all. If you add your own free key (Groq, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Mistral or Cerebras), the tool can also draft and revise. The key stays in your browser and requests go straight to that provider.
    Where is my work saved?
    In this browser only (local storage), until you copy or download it. Nothing you type is sent to Open Practice.
