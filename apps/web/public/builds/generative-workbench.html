ACTIVITY 1 / 35 MIN ESTIMATED

Freeze the source and rubric

Generation is a candidate-making step, not verification. Fix a brief and factual source before changing prompts. A more vivid sentence can score worse if it adds an unsupported price or guarantee. The supplied candidates are authored examples, not claimed model runs. Choose the factual boundary and scoring rule before comparing prompts. Inspect the supplied input before using any tool. Write the decision rules in your own words and calculate one small result independently. The reference output is an answer key for this deliberately bounded fixture, not proof of general capability. Policy for this project: Score factual grounding, required structure and prohibited claims independently. Real provider results must be labelled with date and model; no-service examples stay labelled constructed. For learners across regions underserved or excluded by mainstream providers: verify country eligibility, language, account/identity requirements, data terms, cost, quota and connectivity before choosing a cloud service. Use only legitimate available alternatives; never bypass restrictions or invent a country/account. No service access? Complete the labelled manual/fixture route without claiming a live AI run.

Your practical task

  1. Choose the factual boundary and scoring rule before comparing prompts.
  2. Open input.json and expected.json from the practice pack; keep an unmodified original.
  3. Record a manual prediction and the relevant rule before consulting the answer key.
  4. Perform one small change and predict its effect before rerunning.
  5. Keep the result and your reasoning in the lesson notes; do not mark a simulated run as live.
