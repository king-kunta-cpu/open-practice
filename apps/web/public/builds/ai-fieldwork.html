ACTIVITY 1 / 35 MIN ESTIMATED

Choose a reversible task

A useful introduction to AI starts with a bounded task and an observable failure. Measure checking and rework as well as first-draft speed. One small successful example does not establish general reasoning, reliability across languages or suitability for consequential decisions. Pick a task with a known answer and no private or consequential data. Inspect the supplied input before using any tool. Write the decision rules in your own words and calculate one small result independently. The reference output is an answer key for this deliberately bounded fixture, not proof of general capability. Policy for this project: Count full workflow time. Claims must carry evidence and uncertainty. Simulated customers and constructed outputs are not real validation. For learners across regions underserved or excluded by mainstream providers: verify country eligibility, language, account/identity requirements, data terms, cost, quota and connectivity before choosing a cloud service. Use only legitimate available alternatives; never bypass restrictions or invent a country/account. No service access? Complete the labelled manual/fixture route without claiming a live AI run. Supporting practice — Everyday applications with boundaries: Map one task each in writing, data and vision. Name a useful output and a failure that would matter to its user. Healthcare diagnosis, consequential financial decisions and autonomous actions are outside this beginner exercise. Make a three-row use-case map with a human owner and an explicit stop condition.
