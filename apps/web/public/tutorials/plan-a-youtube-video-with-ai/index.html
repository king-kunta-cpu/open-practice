How to plan a YouTube video with AI

Plan a YouTube video with free AI: pick a title, write a description with chapters, and script the first 30 seconds.

Steps

  1. Write the one promise your video makes.
  2. Generate title options and pick two to test.
  3. Write the description with the YouTube Description tool.
  4. Script the hook with the Viral Hook Generator.
  5. Film, then check the video delivers the title’s promise.
  6. Publish and review click-through after 48 hours.

Open the YouTube Title Generator →

Why this works

Every step ends with something you can check. AI speeds up the draft; your brief and your checks make the result good.
