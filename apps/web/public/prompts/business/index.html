6 business prompts to practise with

Copy a prompt, replace the [BRACKETS] with your details, run it in any AI tool, then check the result. Each prompt includes the habit that makes it reliable.

1Customer interview questions

I sell [PRODUCT] to [CUSTOMER]. Write 8 open interview questions about their last purchase decision. No leading questions, no questions about the future.

Make it reliable: Past behaviour predicts better than opinions.

2Pricing sanity check

My costs per unit are [COSTS]. Competitors charge [PRICES]. Show cost-plus, competitor-based and value-based prices in a table with the assumption behind each. Do not recommend one; list what I should test.

Make it reliable: Prices are hypotheses — test them.

3One-page plan

Create a one-page plan for [IDEA]: problem, customer, offer, price, first 10 customers (where to find them), costs for 3 months, and the one metric that shows it works.

Make it reliable: If you cannot name where the first 10 customers are, start there.

4Complaint reply

Draft a reply to this complaint. Acknowledge the specific problem, say what we will do and by when, and offer one concrete remedy. Under 120 words, no blame, no jargon.

COMPLAINT:
[PASTE]

Make it reliable: Never promise what you cannot deliver.

5Risks list

List the top 8 risks for [PROJECT] in a table: risk, likelihood (L/M/H), impact (L/M/H), early warning sign, and one cheap way to reduce it.

Make it reliable: Early warning signs are the most useful column.

6Meeting summary

Turn these meeting notes into: decisions (with owner), actions (owner + date), open questions. Do not add anything not in the notes.

NOTES:
[PASTE]

Make it reliable: “Do not add anything” reduces invented actions.
