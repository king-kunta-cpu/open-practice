6 learning prompts to practise with

Copy a prompt, replace the [BRACKETS] with your details, run it in any AI tool, then check the result. Each prompt includes the habit that makes it reliable.

1Explain then quiz

Teach me [TOPIC] in 5 short steps, then give me 5 quiz questions. Wait for my answers before revealing the correct ones.

Make it reliable: “Wait for my answers” turns reading into practice.

2Learning plan

Make a 14-day plan to learn [SKILL] in 30 minutes a day. Each day: one concept, one practice task, one way to check I did it right.

Make it reliable: A check for each day keeps you honest.

3Feynman check

Here is my explanation of [CONCEPT]: [MY EXPLANATION]. Point out gaps or errors, then ask me one question that would reveal if I really understand it.

Make it reliable: Explaining is the best test of understanding.

4Worked example

Show a fully worked example of [PROBLEM TYPE], explaining why each step is taken. Then give me a similar problem to try myself.

Make it reliable: Do the second problem without looking.

5Common mistakes

What are the 7 most common beginner mistakes in [SKILL]? For each, show a short wrong example and the corrected version.

Make it reliable: Mistake lists save hours of trial and error.

6Flashcards

Create 15 flashcards for [TOPIC] as “Q: … | A: …”. One fact per card. Include 3 cards that test application, not just recall.

Make it reliable: Paste into any flashcard app.
