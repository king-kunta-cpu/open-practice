6 writing prompts to practise with

Copy a prompt, replace the [BRACKETS] with your details, run it in any AI tool, then check the result. Each prompt includes the habit that makes it reliable.

1Clear rewrite

Rewrite the text below for [READER]. Keep every fact, cut filler, use sentences under 20 words, and keep my voice. Then list any sentence you changed meaning in.

TEXT:
[PASTE]

Make it reliable: Ask for changes to be listed so you can check nothing important was lost.

2Outline first

I am writing a [TYPE] about [TOPIC] for [READER]. Give me an outline with 5 headings, one sentence under each saying what the section proves, and the one example each section needs.

Make it reliable: Outlines are easier to fix than finished drafts.

3Feedback like an editor

Act as a strict editor. Review the draft below for clarity, structure and unsupported claims. Give 5 numbered fixes, most important first. Do not rewrite it.

DRAFT:
[PASTE]

Make it reliable: Asking not to rewrite keeps the work yours.

4Make it shorter

Cut the text below to [N] words without losing any number, name, date or request. Show the word count at the end.

[PASTE]

Make it reliable: Check the count yourself — models miscount.

5Plain-language explainer

Explain [CONCEPT] to someone with no background, in 120 words, using one everyday analogy and one real example from [PLACE/INDUSTRY].

Make it reliable: Local examples make explanations stick.

6Headline options

Write 10 headlines for this article. Mix: 3 how-to, 3 number-led, 2 question, 2 outcome-led. Max 60 characters each. Mark the one you would test first and why.

[SUMMARY]

Make it reliable: Pick two and test them with real readers.
