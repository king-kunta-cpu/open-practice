PROMPT LIBRARY · EVERYDAY

6 everyday prompts to practise with

Copy a prompt, replace the [BRACKETS] with your details, run it in any AI tool, then check the result. Each prompt includes the habit that makes it reliable.

1Explain a bill

Explain the charges on this bill in plain language and list questions I should ask the provider. Remove account numbers first.

[REDACTED BILL]

Make it reliable: Always redact account numbers.

2Meal plan on a budget

Plan 7 dinners for [N] people on about [BUDGET] using local ingredients like [INGREDIENTS]. Include a shopping list grouped by shop section.

Make it reliable: Check prices at your local shop.

3Travel checklist

Create a packing and planning checklist for [TRIP] in [MONTH], including documents, money, health and offline backups.

Make it reliable: Confirm visa and entry rules on official sites.

4Polite but firm

Help me say no to [REQUEST] politely but firmly in under 60 words, offering [ALTERNATIVE] if any.

Make it reliable: Short is kinder than vague.

5Fix-it guide

My [DEVICE] has [PROBLEM]. Give safe troubleshooting steps from easiest to hardest, and tell me when to stop and call a professional.

Make it reliable: Stop at anything involving mains electricity or gas.

6Gift ideas

Suggest 10 gift ideas for [PERSON] who likes [INTERESTS], with budget [AMOUNT]. Mix things to buy, make and experience.

Make it reliable: Handmade gifts are often remembered longest.
