6 marketing prompts to practise with

Copy a prompt, replace the [BRACKETS] with your details, run it in any AI tool, then check the result. Each prompt includes the habit that makes it reliable.

1Audience pains

List 10 specific frustrations [AUDIENCE] has with [PROBLEM], in their own words, as they might say them to a friend. No marketing language.

Make it reliable: Use the best lines as hooks.

2Content calendar

Make a 4-week content plan for [BUSINESS] on [PLATFORM]: 3 posts a week, each with a hook, the format, and the single action we want. Mix education, proof and offers 3:2:1.

Make it reliable: Proof posts need real proof — screenshots, photos, results.

3Offer clarity test

Here is my offer: [OFFER]. Answer as a sceptical customer: what is unclear, what is risky, and what would make you say yes today? Then rewrite the offer in 50 words.

Make it reliable: Fix the objections in the offer, not just the copy.

4Repurpose

Turn this article into: 1 LinkedIn post (150 words), 3 tweets, 1 short-video script (45 seconds) and 1 email subject line. Keep the main claim identical.

[ARTICLE]

Make it reliable: One idea, many formats.

5Ad variations

Write 6 ad variations for [PRODUCT] for [AUDIENCE]: 2 pain-led, 2 benefit-led, 2 proof-led. Headline max 40 characters, body max 125. No claims I cannot prove.

Make it reliable: Run two at a time; keep the winner.

6Landing page review

Review this landing page text. Is it clear within 5 seconds who it is for, what it does and what to do next? Give a score out of 10 for each and three fixes.

[PASTE]

Make it reliable: Clarity beats cleverness.
