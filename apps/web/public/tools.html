A useful list needs boundaries.

The pasted list included Google Teachable Machine, Lobe and TensorFlow Playground. Browser-local training and simulation do not fit this platform’s cloud-inference approach; Lobe is also no longer under active development. Groq, Google AI Studio and Cloudflare Workers AI replace those three entries. Hosted classification is clearly distinguished from training your own model.

“Free chat” does not mean “free API.” Image credits do not unlock every editing feature. A manual workflow or layout is a legitimate learning route, but is not evidence of a live AI run. Opening a tool does not mark an activity complete.
