KIDS · WRITING

Plan a four-panel comic

Plan each panel: what we see and what characters say. Then grab paper and draw.

⏱ 10–20 min · Template only, no AI · No signup

You will practise

  • Planning scenes
  • Writing short dialogue
  • Building to a punchline

Template only — made for young learners. No AI and no signup: your ideas fill the blanks. Best with a grown-up nearby.

  1. 1Brief

    Specific answers make better results — with or without AI.

  2. 2Make

    The template gives you a structure with [[blanks]]. Replace each one with your own ideas.

  3. 3Check

    The same rules run on every draft, human or AI. Most first drafts fail something — that is the point.

      Self-check — judgement no checker can make

    • 4Improve

      Fix one item at a time, then check again. Notice which fixes made it better — that is the skill.

      1. Run the check first.

    • 5Ship

      Use it for real: send it, post it or save it. Record what happened — that is your evidence of learning.

    Pro moves

    • Keep speech to under 10 words per bubble.
    • Draw big faces to show feelings.
    • Sound effects are fun: BOING!

    Questions people ask

    Do I need to draw well?

    No! Stick figures are perfect. The story matters most.
