LEARN · KIDS + A GROWN-UP

Create, imagine and think like a scientist

Six make-it-yourself templates for stories, poems, comics, quizzes, science and kindness. No AI, no signup, no ads — just your ideas, with a grown-up nearby.

For grown-ups

Most AI chat services require users to be 13 or older (some 18). These templates teach the thinking behind good prompts — planning, describing, checking facts — without an AI account. When your child is old enough, the beginner lessons are the next step.

🐉MAKE ITStory BuilderA story template for kids: pick a hero, a place and a problem, then write the story yourself. No AI, no signup — just your imagination.🌈MAKE ITPoem MakerWrite your own poem using fun patterns: acrostic, five senses or rhyming pairs. Kids fill in the template; a grown-up can help read it.🔬MAKE ITScience Question JournalTurn a “why?” question into a mini experiment: guess, test, observe and explain. A kid-friendly science journal template.❓MAKE ITQuiz MakerMake a five-question quiz about something you love. Write questions, answers and a fun fact. Great for family game night.💥MAKE ITComic ScriptPlan a four-panel comic strip: characters, setting, speech bubbles and a funny ending. Then draw it on paper!💛MAKE ITKindness NoteWrite a kind, specific note to thank or cheer up someone — a teacher, friend or family member. Specific praise means the most.

Questions people ask

What age is this for?

Roughly 7–12 with a grown-up. Younger children can dictate while an adult types.

Is any data collected?

No. Everything stays in your browser. There is nothing to sign up for.
