What AI is — and what it is not

AI chat tools predict likely next words from patterns in text. That makes them fast and fluent — and sometimes confidently wrong. Seeing this for yourself is the most useful first lesson.

Do it now

  1. Open any free AI chat you can use (or the prompt box in one of our free tools).
  2. Ask: “What are three good ways to save money on food in my city?” Note the answer.
  3. Open a new chat and ask exactly the same question again.
  4. Compare: what stayed the same, what changed, and which claims could you check?
  5. Write one sentence: “AI is good for … but I must check …”.

Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then open each one.

Why can two answers to the same question differ?

The model samples likely words; small randomness changes the output. Neither answer is “looked up”.

What should you check first?

Numbers, names, dates, prices, laws and anything you will act on.

Tip

Treat AI like a quick, well-read helper who sometimes guesses. Useful — never the final word.
