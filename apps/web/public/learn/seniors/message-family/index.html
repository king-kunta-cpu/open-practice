Write a warm message to family

Sometimes the hardest part is starting. A guided template gives you a structure; your own words make it personal.

Do it now

  1. Choose who you are writing to and why.
  2. Open the Message to Family helper.
  3. Fill in two or three details only you know.
  4. Use the template and replace each [[blank]] with your own words.
  5. Read it aloud, then send it.

Open the Message to Family helper →

Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then open each one.

What makes a message feel personal?

A shared memory or a specific detail only you would know.

Should AI write the whole message?

It can help you start, but your own words matter most.

Tip

A short message sent today is better than a perfect one never sent.
