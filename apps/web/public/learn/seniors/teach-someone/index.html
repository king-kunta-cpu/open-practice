AI FOR SENIORS · LESSON 10 OF 10 · 15 MIN

Teach someone what you learned

Teaching helps you remember and helps your friends stay safe online too.

Do it now

  1. Pick one thing you learned here (for example, the family code word).
  2. Explain it in your own words in three sentences.
  3. Show the person how, step by step.
  4. Let them try while you watch.
  5. Ask them to teach it to someone else.

Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then open each one.

Why does teaching help you learn?

Explaining forces you to understand and remember it.

What is the most important safety lesson to share?

Slow down, and verify any urgent request using a number you already know.

Tip

You are now the expert for someone else. That is a real gift.
