AI for seniors — in large print, at your pace

Ten calm lessons and six guided helpers. Learn what AI can do, how to stay safe from scams, and how to use it for letters, family messages and appointments.

The one rule

If anyone — even a familiar voice — asks for money or codes urgently, hang up and call them back on a number you already know.

Questions people ask

Is AI safe for older adults?

Yes, with a few habits: never share codes or passwords, verify urgent requests with a call back, and check health or money advice with a professional.

Can a family member help me?

Please involve them. Each lesson takes 10–15 minutes and works well side by side.
