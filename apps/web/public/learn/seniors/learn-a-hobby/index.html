AI FOR SENIORS · LESSON 9 OF 10 · 15 MIN

Learn a new hobby with AI

AI can explain things as many times as you like, at your pace, which makes it a wonderful learning companion.

Do it now

  1. Choose a hobby: gardening, a language, local history, cooking.
  2. Ask: “Make me a 4-week beginner plan, 15 minutes a day, in simple words.”
  3. Do day one today.
  4. Ask follow-up questions whenever you are stuck.
  5. Share what you made or learned with someone.

Adapt a recipe with the guided helper →

Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then open each one.

How can you make an AI plan easier?

Ask for shorter steps, simpler words or fewer days.

Should you trust gardening or health tips without checking?

Check important advice with a reliable source or expert.

Tip

Learning something new is one of the best things for the mind.
