SENIORS · GUIDED HELPERS

Guided helpers for everyday tasks

Each helper gives you a simple template. Fill in a few details, replace the blanks with your own words, then check and use it. AI is optional.

💌HELPERMessage to FamilyA large-print guided helper for warm, personal messages to family and friends: birthdays, news, thank-yous or simply keeping in touch.🛡️HELPERScam Message CheckerPractise spotting scam warning signs in texts, emails and calls with a large-print checklist. Learn the safe next step every time.🩺HELPERDoctor Visit QuestionsOrganise symptoms, medicines and questions on one page before an appointment, in large print. It helps you remember; it does not give medical advice.✉️HELPERFormal Letter HelperWrite a polite, clear letter to a bank, council, landlord or company: one request, the facts, and what you want them to do.🍲HELPERRecipe AdapterChange a recipe for fewer people, different ingredients or a softer diet, with clear steps in large print. Always check any medical diet advice.📖HELPERLife Story HelperTurn a photo or memory into a short story your family can keep: who, where, when, what happened and why it mattered.
