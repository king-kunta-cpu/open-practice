Spot AI scams and fake voices

Scammers now use AI to copy voices and write convincing messages. A few simple habits stop almost all of these scams.

Do it now

  1. Agree a family code word that only your family knows.
  2. If someone calls asking for money urgently — even if the voice sounds right — hang up.
  3. Call the person back on the number you already have.
  4. Never pay with gift cards, crypto or wire transfers because a caller asked.
  5. Practise with the Scam Message Checker.

Practise with the Scam Message Checker →

Check yourself

Answer in your head first, then open each one.

A caller sounds like your grandson and needs money now. What do you do?

Hang up and call him back on his usual number, or ask for the code word.

Do banks ask for your PIN by phone or text?

No. Never share a PIN or one-time code.

Tip

Urgency is the scammer’s favourite tool. Slowing down is your best defence.
