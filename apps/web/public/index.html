A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Before you begin.

Is the learning material really free?

Yes. The activities, original handbooks and practice packs are free to read and download. No payment is required. External AI providers may have their own quotas, costs and terms.

Can I learn without an account?

Yes. Explore activities and keep local notes without signing in. An account is needed for cross-device cloud saves and the learner-owned provider connection. Browser drafts do not automatically sync.

What can I use offline?

Download a standalone HTML workspace and the separate practice pack before disconnecting. The HTML includes activities, editable notes, review questions and the capstone rubric. Keep edits by downloading an updated copy. Live cloud inference requires connectivity.

Is this an accredited qualification?

No. Open Practice is independent, non-degree and non-accredited. Progress and rubric scores are learner self-reports. Keep your actual project evidence; do not present the completion indicator as verified certification.
