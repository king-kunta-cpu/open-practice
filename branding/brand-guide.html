Archivo Black for the school's editorial/academic voice. Courier New for technical marginalia. Arial for clear reading copy. Archivo Black is self-hosted under its SIL Open Font License; no font CDN requests.

The visual language

  • A book-and-circuit emblem in a brass gear ring.
  • Technical plates, drafting rules, instrument ticks and restrained teal signals.
  • A workshop index instead of a SaaS feature-card grid.
  • Parchment lesson pages with dark ink, and a dark technical workbench around them.
  • No heavy animation, decorative trackers, font CDNs or visual libraries.

Using the identity

Use the full lockup on dark backgrounds; use the emblem at small sizes. Keep clear space around the mark. Do not stretch the logo or add glows. The supplied masters are transparent raster PNGs; optimised WebP and SVG technical artwork are included.

The logo is AI-generated and selected by the school owner. Trademark/uniqueness clearance has not been performed. Independent and non-accredited—never imply institutional certification.
