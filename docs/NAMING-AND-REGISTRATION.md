# Naming, registration and accreditation

Proposed public brand: **Open Practice AI Engineering School**.

The name describes the subject and learning model; it is not a claim of recognised degrees, professional-engineer licensing, government registration or accreditation. Public pages must keep the independent/non-degree/non-accredited disclosures prominent. No such official status is claimed or verified by this build.

However, a disclaimer is not a legal exemption. Whether the operation must register depends on the jurisdiction, services, audience and funding/fee model, not only whether the word “school” appears in the name. Registration/permission to operate and accreditation/recognition of programmes are separate questions.

If operated from Zimbabwe, the Education Act [Chapter 25:04] contains non-Government school registration provisions (section 15) and correspondence/independent college provisions (Part X, including sections 39–43). The published text includes correspondence tuition “for reward” and restrictions on unregistered provision/advertising. Whether modern online adult practical-AI learning falls within a particular category needs a qualified local assessment. Other laws may apply, including higher education, business registration, privacy, consumer and tax rules.

Sources consulted on 2026-10-21:
- https://zimlii.org/akn/zw/act/1987/5/eng@2020-03-06
- https://natlex.ilo.org/dyn/natlex2/natlex2/files/download/109607/ZWE109607.pdf

These are published statutory texts, not confirmation that every subsequent amendment or administrative interpretation has been captured. Ask the relevant authority or qualified Zimbabwean counsel to confirm the current position before charging, advertising recognised qualifications, or relying on an exemption. This note is informational, not a legal opinion.

If the name's treatment remains unresolved, a temporary public descriptor such as “Open Practice — Practical AI Learning Lab” may reduce confusion, but renaming alone still does not establish a regulatory exemption. The requested School brand has been implemented without implying it is legally cleared.
